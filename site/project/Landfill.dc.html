LANDFILL COSTS

What will my landfill cost? Real loads, real weights.

Every load below is a real Snowy Skips job, photographed at the landfill and weighed on the council weighbridge. The price on each photo is what that load would cost in landfill fees today.

How we charge — the fairest way

Your bin hire price covers the bin, delivery and pickup. Landfill is charged separately, at cost: your load is weighed at the landfill and you pay the council's rate on that weight — nothing added. You get the weighbridge docket, so you're only charged for exactly what you got rid of.

Why not an all-in price?

Some operators build a set weight or a minimum landfill charge into their price. If your load is light — and plenty are — you can end up paying for weight you never used. With us, a light load costs less: a small 1 m³ household load at 0.10 t is about $27 in landfill.

It's the weight, not the size

A 12 m³ load of timber and cabinetry weighed 1.04 t. A 5 m³ load of carpet underlay and synthetic turf weighed 2.2 t. Heavy, wet or dense material costs more — if you're unsure, send us a photo and we'll give you an idea.

How these figures are worked out. Weights are from Snowy Skips weighbridge dockets. Each load has been re-priced at Snowy Monaro Regional Council's current 2026–27 commercial landfill rates (GST inclusive): general waste $270/t, building & demolition waste $320/t, concrete & bricks $99/t, green waste $95/t. On the day, the waste category and rate are set by the landfill at the weighbridge. Clean scrap steel is free at the landfill — keep it separate and it costs nothing to tip. Older loads were charged less at the time — these are today's prices. Council rates change every July; see the council's landfill fees. Items that have to be separated at the landfill (steel, fridges and other white goods, mattresses, tyres, paint) attract a $49.50 separation fee*: for driver safety, the bin has to be placed on the ground in the separate area, the items taken off the top, and the bin pulled back onto the truck before it goes to the tip face. Mixed or rejected loads also attract extra charges — see extra charges.

* Separation fee ($49.50 inc GST per separation): when steel, white goods or other items have to go to a separate area at the landfill, it isn't safe for the driver to pull them off the top of a loaded bin. Our driver unloads the bin and places it on the ground in the steel or white goods area, the items are safely separated off the top, and the bin is pulled back onto the truck and taken up to the tip face. The fee covers that extra unload and reload, done for the driver's safety. The item's own council landfill fee also applies.
