SOIL, FILL AND VENM

Soil is classed as waste. We move it properly.

We're experts in carting and disposing of virgin excavated natural material (VENM) in skip bins and on our trucks right across the Monaro. Soil generally needs testing before it moves — for contamination and weeds — and every load we cart is classified, docketed and tracked from your site to where it ends up.

Accepting soil? Know where it's from and what's in it — including weeds. If a contractor brings you soil, ask for a VENM docket that's fully filled out and signed. Contaminated fill can leave you with the clean-up bill.
