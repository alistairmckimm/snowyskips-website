We started Snowy Skips to give the region a skip bin service that turns up when it says it will and does the job properly. Ten years on, that hasn't changed. We know the roads, the landfills and the weather up here, and we know that a job on a steep Jindabyne block or at a resort in winter takes a bit more planning.

We're more than skip bins. Every job runs on the same SAFETY, QUALITY and ENVIRONMENTAL systems we use on our McKimm Civil construction sites, and every load is tracked from collection to disposal. It's weighed at the landfill and you get the docket, so you know exactly where your waste went and you only pay for what you got rid of.

Our own crew and machines can load anything for you, and we hire out the small plant you'd expect a civil contractor to own. Snowy Skips is part of McKimm Civil Pty Ltd, in earthmoving and civil construction since 2010.
