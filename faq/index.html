FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Skip bin questions, answered

Sizes, prices, landfill, permits and what can go in. Can't see your question? Call us on 0408 632 007.

What size skip bins do you have?

We run bins from 1.5 m³ right through to 12 m³. Our most popular sizes are the 2 m³ mini skip, the 5 m³ renovation skip and the 8 m³ builder's skip. For big jobs our crew can hand load straight into our semi tipper trailer, over 20 m³ in one go. Not sure which size? Send us a photo of what's going and we'll tell you. See skip bin sizes.

How much does a skip bin cost?

Prices start from $330 inc GST for a 2 m³ bin in Cooma, for up to 7 days' hire, plus landfill at cost. Berridale, Jindabyne and other areas cost a little more depending on the distance. Prices on the website are a guide only. If our trucks are already working in your area and you can be flexible on times, we can often give you a sharper quote. See prices for your area.

How is the landfill fee worked out?

Landfill is charged on top of the bin hire, at cost, at the council's current rates. Every load is weighed on the landfill weighbridge and you get the docket. As a rough guide, a 2 m³ household clean-out we weighed at 0.24 t cost about $65 to tip, and a 5 m³ load of timber and building waste at 1.4 t cost about $450. See real loads and what they cost.

How long can I keep the bin?

Prices cover up to 7 days. After that it's $15 a day for a 2 m³ bin, $20 a day for a 5 m³ bin and $25 a day for an 8 m³ bin. Building sites can ask about a permanent bin on a site account.

How quickly can you deliver a skip bin?

We can generally deliver and pick up within 24 hours' notice, but ideally book well ahead so your project runs smoothly. ASAP and emergency jobs may cost extra.

What is a 24-hour hotshot?

A quick turnaround: we drop the bin off and pick it up the next day. Hotshot prices are generally 10% less than standard hire, and up to 15% less if our trucks are already nearby. Hotshot discounts don't apply below our minimum charge.

What can go in a skip bin?

General household rubbish, building, renovation and demolition waste, timber, plasterboard, carpet, underlay, synthetic turf, furniture, and green waste and garden prunings. Concrete, bricks and rubble can go in too, but they're heavy, so don't overfill. Green waste, concrete and clean scrap steel are much cheaper to tip on their own, so ask us about a separate bin. Read the Skip Bin Guide.

What can't go in a skip bin?

Never put asbestos, liquids or sludge, food waste, medical waste and sharps, or animal carcasses in a skip bin. Contact us and we can guide you in the right direction to have these correctly and lawfully serviced.

Can I put a mattress, fridge or tyres in the bin?

Ask us first. Fridges, freezers, air-conditioners, mattresses, tyres, batteries, paint, gas bottles, chemicals, TVs and electronics go to their own area at the landfill. Place them on top of the load so they can be separated. A separation fee of $49.50 inc GST and the item's own council fee apply. See extra charges.

Can you pick up just one item, like a fridge or a mattress?

Yes. You don't need a whole skip bin for one fridge, a mattress, a couch or a desk. If our trucks are in your area, a single item pickup is generally $49 plus the landfill fee for the item. Further out, the price depends on the distance and where our trucks are running, so call us for a quote. See single item pickups.

Do I need a council permit for a skip bin?

On your own property (driveway, yard or building site) you generally don't need one. If the bin has to go on the road, nature strip or footpath, you need Snowy Monaro Regional Council's approval under Section 138 of the Roads Act first. We can handle the application for you: we hold $20 million public liability insurance and have the safe work method statements, and an accredited traffic control company prepares the traffic plan. Allow time for council to approve it. Read council permits for skip bins.

How full can I fill the bin?

Level with the top or below. Nothing over the sides or sticking up, because an overloaded bin can't be carried safely or legally until it's levelled off. Soil, concrete, bricks and rock are extremely heavy, so a bin can be over the legal weight long before it's full. Ask us to put a mark inside your bin showing how high you can fill it. See loading tips.

Can you load the bin for me?

Yes. You don't have to load a bin at all. Our crew and machines can come to your site, load it, separate it and take it right through to the landfill. Send us a few clear photos of everything that's going and we can generally quote the whole job, bin and loading together. See labour hire and clean-outs.

What areas do you service?

We're based in Berridale and service the whole Snowy Monaro: Cooma, Polo Flat, Berridale, Jindabyne, East Jindabyne, Kalkite, Dalgety, Crackenback, Adaminaby, Bredbo, Numeralla, Nimmitabel, Bombala, Delegate, Michelago and more. Thredbo, Perisher, Charlotte Pass and the alpine resorts are by quote, because access can be difficult. We also go further out by quote: the Upper Murray around Khancoban, Corryong and Walwa, and East Gippsland around Bendoc and Cann River. See service areas and zones.

Can you take soil, fill or VENM?

Yes. Under NSW law excavated soil is classed as waste, even when it's clean. We cart and dispose of virgin excavated natural material (VENM) in our skip bins and trucks with the right paperwork and full tracking. Soil generally needs to be tested before it's moved, and it must be kept clean and never mixed with other waste. See how we handle soil and VENM.

Do you price match?

Yes. Send us a genuine written quote or published price from a local operator servicing the Snowy Monaro, dated within the last 7 days, for the same job, before you book, and we'll match it. See how our price match works.

What happens to my waste?

Every bin we run is documented from order to landfill. Your load is weighed, tipped at a landfill or recycling centre that can lawfully accept it, and you get a docket with the weight, tip receipt and photos.
