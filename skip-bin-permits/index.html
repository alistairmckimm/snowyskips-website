SKIP BIN GUIDE

Do I need a council permit for a skip bin in the Snowy Monaro?

The short answer: if the bin goes on your own property (your driveway, yard or building site), you generally don't need a permit. If it has to go on the road, nature strip or footpath, you need Snowy Monaro Regional Council's approval first, and we can look after that for you.

On your own property

Most of our bins go on driveways. We need firm, level ground, clear access for the truck and no low wires overhead. If the bin or truck has to go over grass or a finished surface, tell us when you book and we'll work out the best spot with you. Our 2 m³ mini skip suits tight driveways and town blocks.

On the road, nature strip or footpath

The road reserve (the road, kerb, nature strip and footpath) is controlled by council. Under Section 138 of the NSW Roads Act, any activity in the road reserve needs council's consent before it happens, and Snowy Monaro Regional Council lists skip bins among the activities that need a road management approval.

The application is lodged through the NSW Planning Portal. Council asks for:

  • public liability insurance of at least $20 million, in the applicant's name, with council noted as an interested party
  • a traffic control plan prepared by an accredited person
  • a safe work method statement and details of the work
  • council's application fee, as set in its Fees and Charges

We can do the paperwork for you

Snowy Skips holds $20 million public liability and professional indemnity insurance, and we already have the safe work method statements. Our traffic control plans are prepared by an accredited traffic control company in Jindabyne, usually within about 2 days. So we can apply for the permit on your behalf and look after the whole job, from the application to delivery, pickup and disposal.

The approval itself depends on council's processing time, so let us know as early as you can. Where it makes sense, we'll still look for a spot on your property first, because it's quicker and cheaper for you.

Not sure where your bin can go?

Send us a photo of the spot, or call us on 0408 632 007, and we'll tell you whether it needs a permit.

This is general information, not legal advice. Council's requirements can change; check with Snowy Monaro Regional Council for the current rules.
