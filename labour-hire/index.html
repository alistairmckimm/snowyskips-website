FOR LANDLORDS & PROPERTY MANAGERS

Property clean-outs — back to scratch

Tenant left the place wrecked and full of rubbish? We'll clear it out and get it back to scratch, all in one job.

  1. Clear it out — our crew removes the rubbish, furniture and junk, with the bin and machine on site.
  2. Tip it properly — every load tracked to the landfill, dockets supplied.
  3. Clean it up — once it's gutted, we organise the cleaning of the premises so it's ready to inspect, sell or re-let.

Send us photos of each room and we can generally quote the whole job. One call, one invoice.
