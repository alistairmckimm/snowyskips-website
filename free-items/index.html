FREE TO A GOOD HOME

Good gear shouldn't go to landfill

When we pick up furniture, tools, building materials and other items that are still in good shape, we set them aside instead of tipping them. They're free for locals who need them. It keeps good things out of landfill and helps out in tough times.

Available now

Nothing on the list right now. Check back soon, or follow us on Facebook for new items.

How it works

  1. Call or message us on 0408 632 007 with the item you'd like, and tell us a little about what you need it for.
  2. We'll arrange a time for you to collect it from our depot. Please don't turn up unannounced.
  3. Bring a vehicle and enough help to load it safely, or ask us about delivery: from $49 when our trucks are in your area, depending on the size of the item.

The rules

  • For people who'll use them, not resell them. Anyone found reselling items they got from us will be banned from taking free items in future.
  • Items are listed for about 4 weeks. Anything not collected by then goes to the council's reuse centre or is recycled or disposed of properly.
  • One or two items per household at a time, so more people get a go.
  • First in, best dressed. We may hold an item for someone in genuine need.

What we don't give away

  • Gas appliances and anything that runs on fuel
  • Cots, car seats, prams, high chairs and helmets
  • Recalled or damaged items
  • Mattresses and soft furnishings that aren't clean and in good condition
  • Anything that may contain asbestos
  • Electrical items, unless clearly marked as tested, or as "untested, for parts only"
Disclaimer. All items are second-hand and given away free, as is, with no warranty of any kind. We haven't inspected or tested them unless we say so. Electrical items are untested unless clearly marked otherwise: have them tested and tagged by a licensed electrician before you use them. You're responsible for checking an item is safe and suitable before you take it or use it, and for loading and transporting it safely.
