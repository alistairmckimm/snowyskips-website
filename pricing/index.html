Changeovers and multiple bins cost less: swapping a full bin for an empty one, or booking more than one bin on the same run, means less travel and handling for us, so we can usually give you a sharper price. Just ask when you book.

Permanent bins can be much cheaper: for farms, rural properties and long-running sites, a bin can stay on site with no set pickup days. Give us a few days’ notice when it’s ready to empty and we’ll fit it in with our regular runs, which lets us keep the price well down.

New builds without council bins yet: we specialise in small permanent bins for new homes and buildings that don’t have their occupation certificate yet, so council won’t supply wheelie bins. We’ll keep a bin on site and empty it when you call, until your council bins arrive.

Alpine resorts: access can be difficult in some of these areas, so it's best to give us a call and walk us through your project's access and any special requirements.

Extra charges

Separation fee — $49.50 inc GST ($45 + GST) per separation*, plus that item's own council landfill fee.
Why we charge it: steel, fridges and other white goods, mattresses, tyres, paint and the like have to go to their own area at the landfill. It isn't safe for a driver to climb onto a loaded bin and pull items off the top, so for every separation the bin has to come off the truck:
  1. The driver unloads the bin and places it on the ground in the steel or white goods area.
  2. The items are safely separated off the top.
  3. The bin is pulled back onto the truck and taken up to the tip face.
That extra unload and reload, done for the driver's safety, is what the separation fee covers.
Mixed or rejected loads — $380 per tonne + GST, on top of landfill fees, as per the Snowy Skips Skip Bin Guide.
Charged when a load is rejected or has to be sorted at the landfill.

Never in a skip bin: asbestos, liquids and sludge, food waste, medical waste and sharps, animal carcasses. Please contact us and we can guide you in the right direction to have these correctly and lawfully serviced.

Price match — how it works

Found a cheaper skip bin? We'll match it. Just send us the quote before you book and we'll check it.

  • Same job: same bin size (or smaller), same type of waste, same delivery address and a similar hire period.
  • Comparing tip fees: we match the bin hire price. If their price includes tip fees up to a set weight, we'll compare at that weight using the council's current landfill rates. Landfill on your actual load is charged at cost either way.
  • Real and current: a written quote or published price from a local operator servicing the Snowy Monaro, dated within the last 7 days.
  • Before you book: we match when you book, not as a refund afterwards. One match per job.
  • Not included: specials, clearance or one-off deals, trade-account pricing, and bins booked through online marketplaces.
  • Same rules after: our extra charges (separation fees, rejected mixed loads) still apply.

* Separation fee ($49.50 inc GST per separation): when steel, white goods or other items have to go to a separate area at the landfill, it isn't safe for the driver to pull them off the top of a loaded bin. Our driver unloads the bin and places it on the ground in the steel or white goods area, the items are safely separated off the top, and the bin is pulled back onto the truck and taken up to the tip face. The fee covers that extra unload and reload, done for the driver's safety. The item's own council landfill fee also applies.
