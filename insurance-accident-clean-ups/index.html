Storm, fire, flood or accident damage? We clear the site so your builder can start.

24-hour emergency call-outs for storm damage, fallen trees and make-safe work.

Get us in straight away

After a storm, fire or flood, the first few hours matter. The sooner the site is made safe and cleared, the less extra damage there is: loose roofing iron and debris can blow into neighbouring properties and the bush, and water that gets in keeps spreading through floors and walls. Insurers generally expect damage to be kept from getting worse, so call us as soon as it happens, day or night, on 0408 632 007.

What we do

  • Full clean-outs of damaged contents: furniture, carpets, whitegoods, flood-soaked and smoke-damaged items.
  • Strip-outs: wet plasterboard, carpet and underlay, kitchens, flooring and fittings that need to come out before repairs.
  • Small demolition: damaged sheds, carports, garages, decks and small single-storey structures (see the note on demolition below).
  • Make-safe and site clean-up: storm debris, fallen trees (we have a 12-inch tree chipper), broken fencing, and clearing up after a vehicle has gone through a fence, carport or shed.
  • Site set-up for the rebuild: temporary fencing, site toilets, site sheds and containers, and bins for the builder.

Bins, machines and labour all come from us, so there's one booking, one invoice and one set of records.

Records for your insurer or loss adjuster

  • Geotagged, date- and time-stamped photos before, during and after the clean-up, plus video where it helps
  • Live video calls from site with you, your loss adjuster or insurer while the job is under way, so they can see the damage and the work without travelling
  • Weighbridge dockets for every load, with tonnes by waste type
  • A clear, itemised invoice for bins, labour, machinery and landfill
  • A written quote or scope for the assessor before we start, where the claim needs one

Every job goes through our waste tracking, so you can show exactly what was removed and where it went.

We can manage the whole job, from damage to ready-to-build

Snowy Skips can run the entire process for you under one management overlay: making the site safe, the clean-up, the records, and keeping you, your insurer and the loss adjuster up to date. We hand over to your builder when you're ready, either once the site is cleared, or after we've also done the site preparation so it's ready to go. One point of contact from the day of the damage until the rebuild starts.

On this job we managed the whole process: the licensed asbestos removal was completed first, then we carried out the demolition clean-up and cleared the site.

Asbestos comes out first

Many homes built before 1990 contain asbestos, and fire or storm damage can break it up. If asbestos may be present, it must be checked and removed by a licensed asbestos removalist before anyone else works on the site. We don't remove asbestos ourselves, but as part of managing the job we can engage and coordinate a licensed asbestos removalist for you. Once the area has been cleared, we take over the rest of the clean-up. Asbestos can't go in our skip bins.

A note on demolition

We take down small damaged structures that don't need a demolition licence in NSW: generally structures under 6 metres high, with no machinery working on a raised or suspended floor. Anything bigger goes to a licensed demolition contractor, and we can supply the bins, machinery and cartage for them.

How it works

  1. Call us on 0408 632 007. For urgent storm or make-safe work, call any time, day or night.
  2. We look at the site (or your photos) and give you, your builder or the assessor a quote and scope.
  3. We isolate the site and make it safe before anyone goes in: physical barriers and warning signage, a full site safety inspection, and we work alongside licensed contractors who can safely certify that services such as power, gas and water are isolated before entry. These are the same safety systems we use on our McKimm Civil construction sites. Doing this quickly helps prevent further damage to the building's structure, pavements and the rest of the property.
  4. We clear the site, keeping everything separated so recyclable and cheaper-to-tip waste costs less.
  5. You get the photos, dockets and invoice for the claim, and the site is ready for the builder.
