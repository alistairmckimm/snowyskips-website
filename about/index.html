ABOUT US

Ten years of keeping the Snowy Mountains clean

Snowy Skips is locally owned and operated in Berridale. For ten years we've been delivering skip bins, loading jobs and clearing sites right across the Snowy Monaro — from town blocks in Cooma to building sites in Jindabyne and the high country beyond.

We started Snowy Skips to give the region a skip bin service that turns up when it says it will and does the job properly. Ten years on, that hasn't changed. We know the roads, the landfills and the weather up here, and we know that a job on a steep Jindabyne block or at a resort in winter takes a bit more planning.

We're more than skip bins. We uphold high standards of safety, quality and environmental care on every job, with full waste tracking from collection to disposal. Every load is weighed at the landfill and you get the docket — so you know exactly where your waste went, and you only pay for what you got rid of.

Our own crew and machines can load anything for you, and we hire out the small plant you'd expect a civil contractor to own. Snowy Skips is part of McKimm Civil Pty Ltd, and brings the same systems and standards from our civil construction work to every bin we drop off.
