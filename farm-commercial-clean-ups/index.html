FARM & COMMERCIAL CLEAN-UPS

Big clean-up on the farm or at the business? We bring the bins, machines and crew.

Old machinery, sheds, wire, scrap steel, car batteries and years of built-up rubbish. We clear large farm and commercial sites right across the Snowy Monaro, Upper Murray and far East Gippsland.

What we clear

  • Old machinery, vehicles, implements and heavy scrap steel
  • Fencing wire, posts, old yards and collapsed or unwanted sheds
  • Large numbers of car and truck batteries
  • General rubbish, timber, tyres and green waste from around the property
  • Commercial sites: warehouse and shop clean-outs, end-of-lease strip-outs and yard clean-ups
  • Old slabs, footings and concrete: see concrete removal & disposal

Scrap steel and batteries have value

Heavy scrap steel and batteries go to authorised scrap metal recyclers in the cities, not landfill. Because the scrap is worth something, on large jobs the bins and pickup may be free or reduced, depending on how much there is and where the property is. Give us a call and we'll take a look. (This is for big clean-ups only, not small loads of steel.)

Batteries are packed to meet the legal requirements for transport and taken off site separately. They never go in a skip bin.

Machines, crew and bins on site

We bring the excavator or loader, the trucks and bins, and a crew to load it all. While the machine is on site, it can also do other jobs around the property: clearing, tidying up yards, digging or moving material. Ask us when you book.

For ongoing work, a permanent bin on the farm is much cheaper than one-off bins: no set pickups, just give us a few days' notice and we fit it into our regular runs.

Machinery or sheds to move rather than scrap? We also run drop deck floats. See machinery & large item transport.

Every load tracked

You get weighbridge dockets and a record of where everything went: scrap to the recycler, rubbish to landfill. Handy for your records, your accountant or a business's environmental reporting. See waste tracking.
